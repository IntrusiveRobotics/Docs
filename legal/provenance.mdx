The Delta firmware is a derivative of the open source [VESC project](https://github.com/vedderb/bldc) by Benjamin Vedder, adapted by Intrusive Robotics for the Delta GaN ESC hardware.

## Relationship to upstream

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upstream source" icon="code-branch" href="https://github.com/vedderb/bldc">
    `vedderb/bldc` — the VESC firmware the Delta derives from.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Delta source" icon="github" href="https://github.com/IntrusiveRobotics/Delta">
    `IntrusiveRobotics/Delta` — this firmware, including hardware support for the Delta.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The Delta firmware adds hardware support for the Delta GaN ESC — the GaN gate drive stage, thermal management for the 70 A rating, and board-specific pin and peripheral configuration. It remains compatible with VESC Tool for configuration, tuning, and firmware operations.

<Note>
  The Delta is **sensorless only**. There is no encoder input on this hardware, so encoder-related functionality present in upstream VESC does not apply.
</Note>
