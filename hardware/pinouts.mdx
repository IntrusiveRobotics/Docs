This page documents the physical connectors on the Delta GaN ESC (REV A) and what each pin carries.

## Power and phase

<Warning>
  High current and high voltage. The Delta accepts up to **93 V continuous** and delivers up to **70 A continuous** with forced airflow. Never make or break these connections under load, and use an anti-spark connector on the battery side.
</Warning>

## USB

USB-C, **data only**. The Delta is not powered over USB and will not enumerate until DC power is applied to the main input terminals. Use a USB A → USB C cable. See the [quickstart](/guides/quickstart).
